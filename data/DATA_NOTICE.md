# MNIST dataset attribution

MNIST handwritten digits were created by Yann LeCun, Corinna Cortes and
Christopher J. C. Burges from original NIST handwriting data. The original
homepage is http://yann.lecun.com/exdb/mnist/.

Keras's authoritative dataset documentation states that Yann LeCun and Corinna
Cortes hold the MNIST copyright and make it available under Creative Commons
Attribution-Share Alike 3.0:
https://keras.io/api/datasets/mnist/
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/

The four `.gz` files are unmodified MNIST source files downloaded from
https://storage.googleapis.com/cvdf-datasets/mnist/.
The notebook scales and thresholds copies of pixels for this experiment;
it does not replace or modify these source files. Preserve this attribution
and follow the dataset's terms when redistributing it or adapted data.

The data license is not a blanket license for this entire repository, and
does not establish legal ownership of model outputs. Generated figures are
explicitly identified as synthetic and are not handwritten records.
